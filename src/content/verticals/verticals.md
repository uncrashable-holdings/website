**The [AOIDR](/framework/aoidr) framework does not change from one setting to another. What changes is how Invisible Fragility appears. A household depends on different structures than a nonprofit. A privately held business faces different continuity pressures than an athletic career. The framework remains consistent. Its application becomes specific.**

### Homeowner

A household may look organized while one person still carries the accounts, decisions, routines, and knowledge that keep it functioning.

How households continue running without depending on one person to carry everything.

[Explore Homeowner →](/verticals/homeowner)

### Business Owner

A business can look valuable while every major decision, relationship, or process still runs through one person nobody else can replace.

How privately held businesses outgrow operational dependence and owner concentration.

[Explore Business Owner →](/verticals/business-owner)

### Nonprofit

A mission can look secure while one founder, board chair, or funder quietly holds the whole structure together.

How mission-driven organizations continue serving beyond the people currently carrying them.

[Explore Nonprofit →](/verticals/nonprofit)

### Athlete

Performance can create extraordinary opportunity while leaving the athlete's income, decisions, relationships, and future attached to the ability to compete.

How athletic careers become the foundation for lives that outlast competition.
