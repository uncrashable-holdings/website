**The Continuity Stress Test is the investigative instrument of the Uncrashable Methodology. Unlike the Continuity Scan and the Invisible Fragility Index, it does not rely on a fixed question set. It investigates how dependencies interact, why fragility exists, and what structural changes would most strengthen continuity. Its outcome is Continuity Architecture.**

---

## Investigation Instead of Questionnaire

The Continuity Stress Test follows the structure.

Not a script.

Not a score.

Every engagement examines the unique dependencies revealed by earlier diagnostics.

The investigation adapts to the organization.

The methodology does not.

## What the Stress Test Investigates

The Continuity Stress Test explores hidden dependencies.

Decision continuity.

Ownership continuity.

Income continuity.

Knowledge continuity.

Recoverability.

It seeks relationships.

Not isolated answers.

## The Outcome

The purpose of investigation is understanding.

The purpose of understanding is design.

The outcome of the Continuity Stress Test is <a href="/continuity-architecture" class="action">Continuity Architecture</a>.

Recommendations are not generic.

They are built around the structure being examined.

## Who It Is For

The Continuity Stress Test is designed for organizations ready to strengthen continuity intentionally.

It is appropriate when measurement has identified meaningful fragility or when structural understanding matters more than another score.

## What Comes Next

Investigation is not the end of the methodology.

It is the beginning of intentional design.

Continuity Architecture transforms understanding into action.
