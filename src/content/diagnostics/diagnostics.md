**The Uncrashable Diagnostics are a family of assessments built on the AOIDR framework. Each examines the same five pillars of continuity using a different level of depth. The framework does not change. Only the depth of examination changes.**

## One Framework. Three Levels of Examination.

Every diagnostic asks the same underlying question.

What still works when something important changes?

They differ only in how deeply they investigate the answer.

The Continuity Scan begins the conversation.

The Invisible Fragility Index measures the structure.

The Continuity Stress Test investigates the findings.

Different instruments.

One framework.

## Continuity Scan

The Continuity Scan is the first conversation.

Five questions.

Five pillars.

No score.

No diagnosis.

Its purpose is to reveal whether continuity deserves a closer look.

It is designed for people encountering Invisible Fragility for the first time.

If the questions change how you think, it has already done its job.

[Explore the Continuity Scan →](/diagnostics/continuity-scan)

## Invisible Fragility Index

The Invisible Fragility Index is the first measurement.

Twenty-five questions.

Five pillars.

One fragility score.

One pillar profile.

Its purpose is to identify where Invisible Fragility is concentrated and provide a common language for discussing continuity before disruption does.

Measurement comes before interpretation.

Interpretation comes before action.

[Explore the Invisible Fragility Index →](/diagnostics/ifi)

## Continuity Stress Test

The Continuity Stress Test is the first investigation.

No fixed question set.

Structured investigation.

Its purpose is to examine why fragility exists, how dependencies interact, and where recoverability can be strengthened.

It is designed for organizations ready to move beyond measurement into structural understanding.

The goal is [Continuity Architecture](/continuity-architecture).

[Explore the Continuity Stress Test →](/diagnostics/continuity-stress-test)

## Which One Comes First?

Most people begin with the Continuity Scan.

Some organizations begin with the Invisible Fragility Index because they already know continuity deserves attention.

The Continuity Stress Test is designed for situations where understanding the structure matters more than measuring it.

There is no wrong starting point.

There is only the level of examination that best matches the question you are trying to answer.

## The Methodology Remains Constant

Every diagnostic is built on the same methodology.

Every diagnostic examines the same five pillars.

Every diagnostic speaks the same language.

As the depth of examination increases, the framework does not change.

It becomes more detailed.

That consistency allows observations from one level of examination to remain meaningful at every other level.
