**The Invisible Fragility Index is the measurement instrument of the Uncrashable Methodology. Through twenty-five questions across the five AOIDR pillars, it identifies where Invisible Fragility is concentrated and produces a structured continuity profile. The purpose of the IFI is not to predict disruption. It is to measure dependence before disruption reveals it.**

---

## Twenty-Five Questions. One Structure.

Five questions examine each AOIDR pillar.

Twenty-five responses create a single continuity profile.

The result is not simply a score.

It is a structured picture of how continuity depends on Authority, Ownership, Income, Documentation, and Resilience.

## What the IFI Measures

The IFI measures Invisible Fragility.

It identifies concentrations of structural dependence.

It reveals where continuity is strongest.

It reveals where recoverability deserves closer attention.

Measurement creates a common language.

Interpretation creates understanding.

## Who It Is For

The IFI is designed for individuals and organizations ready to move beyond recognition into measurement.

It is appropriate when continuity questions deserve more than instinct.

## What Comes Next

Measurement identifies where fragility exists.

It does not explain why.

When understanding the structure itself becomes important, the <a href="/diagnostics/continuity-stress-test" class="action">Continuity Stress Test</a> becomes the next level of examination.
