**The Continuity Scan is the introductory diagnostic within the Uncrashable Methodology. Through five questions, one from each AOIDR pillar, it reveals whether continuity deserves a closer look. The Continuity Scan does not diagnose Invisible Fragility. It helps determine whether deeper examination is warranted.**

---

## Five Questions. Five Pillars.

The Continuity Scan asks one question from each AOIDR pillar.

Authority.

Ownership.

Income.

Documentation.

Resilience.

Five questions cannot explain a structure.

They can reveal whether one deserves attention.

## What the Scan Does

The Continuity Scan introduces continuity thinking.

It reveals patterns that are often overlooked.

It helps people recognize Invisible Fragility before attempting to measure it.

The goal is not certainty.

The goal is recognition.

## Who It Is For

The Continuity Scan is designed for people encountering Invisible Fragility for the first time.

Families.

Business owners.

Nonprofit leaders.

Athletes.

Anyone wondering whether continuity depends on more than they realized.

## The First Conversation

The Continuity Scan is not a form, and it is not scored.

It is five questions, one from each pillar, asked as the first conversation about what keeps working when circumstances change.

To begin one, [start a conversation](/contact).

## What Comes Next

Some people finish the Scan with greater confidence.

Others discover questions they have never considered.

When the Scan reveals meaningful dependence, the next step is measurement.

The <a href="/diagnostics/ifi" class="action">Invisible Fragility Index</a> provides that deeper examination.

Anyone who prefers to evaluate their own exposure independently can go directly to the Index that fits their situation.
