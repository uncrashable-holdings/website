**Utomy McLamb is the founder of Uncrashable Holdings and a Continuity Architect, the practitioner behind the AOIDR framework and the Invisible Fragility Index. His background includes a Series 65 license, and Uncrashable Holdings operates as a veteran-founded practice pursuing SDVOSB certification.**

---

Before there was AOIDR, there was a question. It came from watching the same pattern repeat across very different situations: a business, a family, a nonprofit, each one stable on the surface, each one quietly dependent on something nobody had written down.

That observation became a question, and the question became a framework. [Invisible Fragility](/framework/invisible-fragility) named the condition. [AOIDR](/framework/aoidr) built the language to examine it.

Uncrashable Holdings exists to apply that framework where it matters: in the structures that hold up families' plans, founders' businesses, nonprofits' missions, athletes' careers, long after the moment that built them has passed.

Continuity comes first. Optimization comes after.
