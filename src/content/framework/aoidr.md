**AOIDR is a continuity framework that evaluates the structural strength of a family, business, or organization across five interconnected pillars: Authority, Ownership, Income, Documentation, and Resilience. Rather than measuring performance or optimization alone, AOIDR examines whether the structure can continue to function when ordinary disruption occurs. It provides a common language for identifying [invisible fragility](/framework/invisible-fragility) before it becomes visible failure.**

Architecture comes before optimization. Always.
