**Invisible Fragility is the hidden dependence on people, knowledge, income, authority, or systems that allows a family, business, or organization to appear stable while becoming increasingly vulnerable to disruption. Unlike obvious risks, invisible fragility often goes unnoticed because day-to-day operations continue to function until a single event exposes the weakness. Identifying and reducing invisible fragility is the purpose of Continuity Architecture and the problem the [AOIDR framework](/framework/aoidr) was designed to diagnose.**

**Risk will always exist. Fragility doesn't have to.**
